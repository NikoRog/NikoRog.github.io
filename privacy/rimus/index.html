Privacy Policy

Rimus

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Rimus is a word and rhyme game. No account is required to play.

Information Rimus does not directly request

Rimus does not directly request your name, email address, phone number, or similar personal information.

Analytics and advertising

Rimus uses Google AdMob to show advertising. Google AdMob may process device identifiers, IP address, app interactions, advertising data, and technical information according to Google’s policies. Rimus does not currently include Firebase Analytics or transmit its local analytics events to a third-party analytics service.

Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and Advertising Policies.

Consent and privacy choices

Rimus uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent where required. When available, you can reopen applicable choices through Privacy Options in Rimus Settings.

Local storage

Rimus may store game settings and preferences locally on your device so the app can remember your choices.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact nikorogulja@gmail.com.
