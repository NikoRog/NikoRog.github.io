About Me

I design gameplay systems, level layouts, and player experiences with a strong focus on game feel, clarity, and engagement. I work hands-on with Unity, Godot, and Blender to prototype, implement, and iterate on core gameplay mechanics. My background in 3D art and animation allows me to create readable, functional, and visually cohesive game experiences that align design, technical execution, and player feedback.
