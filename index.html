About Me

I work across game design and art, moving from raw concept to playable or visual output. I like projects that feel clear, tactile, and memorable.

My process is simple: understand the core idea, build the strongest version of it fast, and iterate only where it matters.

I care about strong direction, readable interaction, and visuals that support the work instead of hiding it.
